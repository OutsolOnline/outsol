## Fixed scope and fees

New launches remain **Pump.fun, Solana and SOL only**. OutSol adds **0% platform fees**, with no subscription. Network fees, protocol charges, rent, Jito tips and third-party service costs remain separate. Local encrypted keys, explicit approval, native packet validation, signed simulation, bounded spending and durable original-signature recovery remain mandatory. None of the additions grants a server custody of local keys or removes normal transaction review.

Checked against the official sources on 3 November: Proxima's fee guide and Terms, last updated 1 November 2026, agree on **2.5% buys, sells and creator-reward claims**, and **0.5% Streamflow locks**. The Volume Bot figures conflict: **0.25% in the guide; 1% per side in Terms §9**. The applicable license and final operation preview govern. The older comparison's 5% sell/claim figures are stale. See [official fee guide](https://docs.proxima.tools/other/platform-fees/) and [Terms §9](https://proxima.tools/terms/). OutSol's dated editorial fee reference is in [feeComparison.ts](../src/lib/feeComparison.ts); it is not an execution quote.

## Native additions present in the branch

- **Mint-address cloning and portable kits.** Verified Token-2022 or SPL Metaplex identity becomes an editable fresh draft. Optional external metadata/artwork reads are bounded, browser-only, CORS-only and omit credentials/redirects. A clone uses fresh defaults and metadata upload, without copying source authorities or wallet assignments. Portable kits can embed a validated raster up to 5 MB, carry public presets and generic role slots, and remove actual wallet/group/fee-recipient bindings. Imports require explicit rebinding before launch. Existing plain-draft files retain their older separate behavior. Sources: [tokenClone.ts](../src/lib/tokenClone.ts), [launchKits.ts](../src/lib/launchKits.ts), [Clone page](../app/clone/page.tsx), [LaunchKitsPanel](../src/components/launch/LaunchKitsPanel.tsx).
- **Deploy now, explicitly buy later.** Creator-only deployment saves public preparation and attaches only its matching confirmed mint. Subsequent single-wallet steps or ordered sequences get fresh review, quotes and simulation, exact debit ceilings and optional newly purchased-token forwarding. Per-wallet amount, delay and listed/reverse order are exposed. Original unknowns stop remaining work; reload cannot arm it. Legacy automatic Manual timing is preserved unless deferral is explicitly selected. Sources: [launchHandoff.ts](../src/lib/buyPlans/launchHandoff.ts), [buy engine](../src/lib/buyPlans/engine.ts), [BuyPlanPanel](../src/components/buyPlans/BuyPlanPanel.tsx).
- **Tabbed token workspace.** Overview, focused trade dialog, staged buys, creator operations, supply actions, receipt accounting, verification, notes and activity are integrated into the token page. A public mint can be opened and saved locally. Sources: [token page](../app/token/[mint]/page.tsx), [tokenProjects.ts](../src/lib/tokenProjects.ts).
- **Creator operations.** Native creator-wallet vault collection, shared-fee distribution and eligible fee-sharing setup/finalization use the official Pump SDK, exact native validation and signed same-bank simulation. Wallet-wide collection can include fees from other tokens and pre-existing wrapped SOL is separated. Larger eligible operations can use a separately reviewed public account lookup setup. Sources: [creator engine](../src/lib/creatorOperations/engine.ts), [native recipes](../src/lib/creatorOperations/native.ts), [CreatorOperationsPanel](../src/components/creatorOperations/CreatorOperationsPanel.tsx).
- **Supply operations.** Checked burn verifies both exact source balance and mint-supply decreases. Percentage-of-source-balance or total-supply distributions convert into explicit transfers to up to eight recipients from one selected source; integer remainders stay with the sender. Sources: [wallet builder](../src/lib/walletOperations/builder.ts), [distribution.ts](../src/lib/walletOperations/distribution.ts), [WalletOperations](../src/components/wallet/WalletOperations.tsx).
- **Consolidate, then sell.** A strict public journal guides transfers into one controlled receiver, followed by a separate fresh sale review for only this journal's exact finalized and root-receipt-proven transferred sum. Pre-existing receiver holdings are not added to the sale amount. Unknown originals block continuation, refresh does not resume, and a journal permits only one sale attempt. The phases are non-atomic: transfers remain in the receiver if sale preparation or execution is unavailable. Sources: [consolidation model](../src/lib/consolidation/model.ts), [engine](../src/lib/consolidation/engine.ts), [panel](../src/components/consolidation/ConsolidateAndSellPanel.tsx).
- **Local organization and preferences.** Arbitrary public-address watchlists have names, markers, tags, bookmarks, token association, import/export and explicit balance inspection. Projects support search/archive/restore and public handoff. Wallet groups have color/archive controls. Funder stores a public default source and exposes reviewed group funding/withdrawal. Settings provide a local name/initials avatar, contrast-checked palettes and configurable review shortcuts. Sources: [Watchlist](../app/watchlist/page.tsx), [Projects](../app/projects/page.tsx), [group preferences](../src/components/wallet/GroupPreferencesPanel.tsx), [Funder](../app/funder/page.tsx), [Settings](../app/settings/page.tsx).

## Working subsets that remain partial

- **Supply targets and scale.** Official-SDK sequential quotes estimate additional creator/holder purchases and separate funding allowances. They do not set guaranteed ownership, calculate maximum loss or promise executable final allocation. Launch/staged plans support **16 buyers, not 200**; immediate Block-0 is at most three, sometimes fewer because of packet limits. See [quotes.ts](../src/lib/buyPlans/quotes.ts) and [launch settings](../src/lib/launchBundle/types.ts).
- **Market data.** Official confirmed Pump bonding-curve events form a bounded recent feed and observed-minute OHLC, with own/watched markers. Event identity uses signature plus log ordinal and failed invocation chains are excluded. Up to 12 recent transactions and 20 largest token accounts are sampled; top accounts are grouped by account authority with explicit sample coverage. There is no complete holder count, beneficial-owner clustering, Bubble Maps, full historical chart, market-wide intelligence feed or **PumpSwap** market index. Missing history is not filled with invented prices. See [tokenMarket.ts](../src/lib/server/tokenMarket.ts), [events.ts](../src/lib/market/events.ts), [market panel](../src/components/market/TokenMarketPanel.tsx).
- **Observed-buy guards and counters.** Cumulative stop rules ignore baseline/own/whitelisted events, deduplicate and trigger only when observed buys strictly exceed the threshold. Unreadable, stale or non-overlapping samples stop future actions. Sampling may miss trades and does not monitor paused time; this is **not instant protection or automatic liquidation**. A counter reacts to a new qualifying external **buy** with one fixed-size reviewed trade; ratio sizing and external-sell triggers are absent. See [observedTrades.ts](../src/lib/tradingSessions/observedTrades.ts), [strategyQueue.ts](../src/lib/tradingSessions/strategyQueue.ts).
- **Automation.** One to ten ordered strategies can span controlled wallets with a shared debit ceiling, at most ten confirmed trades and five active minutes across continuations. DCA, SOL cap steps, confirmed-launch watches and fixed counters are composable within those limits. Pause/edit/continue requires fresh approval; completed identities, reservations and originals are preserved. There are no simultaneous independent orders, USD grid feed, unlimited runtime or browser-closed worker. See [queueModel.ts](../src/lib/tradingSessions/queueModel.ts), [stored-step review](../src/components/tradingSessions/StrategyQueueStepReview.tsx).
- **Accounting.** A bounded persistent local ledger saves verified receipt balance observations; public signatures and discovered local receipts are checked in batches of eight. Cashflow uses actual token/native/WSOL changes reconciled with official events, with network fees and account reserves separate. Quote ceilings are never acquisition costs. **Realized and unrealized P&L remain unavailable without complete history.** Matching sampled endpoint balances cannot prove continuity, so acquisition basis is not carried between receipts. CSV and SVG export evidence, not a fabricated profit card or complete account valuation. See [ledger.ts](../src/lib/accounting/ledger.ts), [fills.ts](../src/lib/accounting/fills.ts), [TokenPerformancePanel](../src/components/market/TokenPerformancePanel.tsx).
- **Creator permissions and permanence.** Ordinary supported collection/distribution is native; social and holder-reward claims still require Pump's external authority flow. Eligible ordinary-wallet shares support one to ten distinct positive recipients totaling 100%; accepting a charity address is not charity verification or donate.gg integration. **Final share assignment permanently revokes the administrator.** Finalized/revoked or v1 distributions cannot be edited. See [creator inspection](../src/lib/creatorOperations/inspect.ts), [Pump's primary fee-sharing specification](https://github.com/pump-fun/pump-public-docs/blob/main/docs/instructions/CREATOR_FEE_SHARING.md).
- **Streamflow escrow.** Implemented post-launch supply locking is a separately reviewed **one-off immutable escrow** with a single future unlock, exact principal and live protocol fees/rent. Supported revoked-authority SPL/Token-2022 mints and source restrictions apply. This is distinct from a launch-time developer lock and from LP locking; post-unlock withdrawal opens Streamflow rather than using an added in-app withdrawal transaction. See [supply lock engine](../src/lib/supplyLocks/engine.ts), [native checks](../src/lib/supplyLocks/native.ts), [TokenLockPanel](../src/components/supply/TokenLockPanel.tsx), [Streamflow lock documentation](https://developers.streamflow.finance/docs/core-concepts/streams/locks).
- **History, recovery and account access.** Central Activity discovers the new public operation journals and their original-signature recovery entries, but retained browser receipts are not an exhaustive chain ledger. Wallet inspection, supported sale/closure and per-operation recovery are not a combined cross-launch recovery wizard. Project exports are public handoffs, not invitations or backend-enforced view/write/admin collaboration. Local name/initials and encrypted vaults are not hosted profiles, 2FA/session management or account-performance analytics. See [workspaceActivity.ts](../src/lib/workspaceActivity.ts), [operationActivity.ts](../src/lib/operationActivity.ts), [tokenProjects.ts](../src/lib/tokenProjects.ts).
- **Execution preferences.** Optional server configuration races the same validated signed packet through up to three verified HTTPS mainnet RPC transports. It preserves the original signature and adds no new intent/amount/tip. It is not a proprietary router, arbitrary relay race or inclusion guarantee, and configured providers are required. Shortcuts only select/focus review controls. See [providerRacing.ts](../src/lib/server/providerRacing.ts), [rpcProxy.ts](../src/lib/server/rpcProxy.ts), [WorkspacePreferencesProvider](../src/providers/WorkspacePreferencesProvider.tsx).

## Absent integrations and deliberate differences

No exposed implementation supplies Proxima's Front/Back/temporary-wallet layers, Quick/Clean/Full layered builds, Husher/CEX settlement, proxy-hop funding tasks, Hushxima aged-wallet inventory, dedicated activity-generation loops, volume-target round trips, post-layer cleanup or proprietary Shield router. No authorized private API, service contract or inventory is assumed. Native primitives are not advertised as those private integrations. Proxima's public docs describe those workflows; they do not provide an implemented OutSol integration. Relevant official references: [funding](https://docs.proxima.tools/wallets/fund-wallets/), [marketplace](https://docs.proxima.tools/wallets/marketplace/), [execution settings](https://docs.proxima.tools/other/settings/).

Unattended signing and writable multi-user collaboration need a separately designed secure account/storage/authorization architecture compatible with local custody. No server-managed signer has been introduced. Additional chains/launchpads/quote currencies, platform-fee referral rewards and instant trade review bypass remain deliberate differences. Public-address Watchlist is separate from the still-coming-soon continuous X tracker.

Existing OutSol extras remain local launch rehearsal, public mint verification, web/X capture, local vanity generation and reviewed multi-launch. Their presence does not establish that Proxima lacks an equivalent feature.
